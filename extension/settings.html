Exit country

Where your traffic leaves the Tor network.

Preferred country Tor will prefer exits in this country. If none are available it will use another.
Picking one country can make you easier to tell apart from other Tor users.

Kill switch

Keeps your real IP hidden if Tor stops working.

Block traffic if Tor disconnects If Tor drops or the extension restarts, your browser traffic stays blocked instead of falling back to your real IP. Press Connect to restore access.
Protected. Your browser traffic never leaves without Tor. If Tor drops you lose internet until you press Connect. That is the point, but it can look like a broken connection.
